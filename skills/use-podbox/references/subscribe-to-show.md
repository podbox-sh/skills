## Apple Podcasts

On iPhone or iPad:

1. Copy the address above.
2. Open Apple Podcasts.
3. Tap Library, then the More button at the top, then Follow a Show by URL.
4. Paste the address and tap Follow.

On Mac:

1. Copy the address above.
2. Open Apple Podcasts.
3. Choose File, then Follow a Show by URL.
4. Paste the address and click Follow.

## Overcast

1. Copy the address above.
2. Open Overcast.
3. Tap the + button, then Add URL.
4. Paste the address and tap Add.

## Pocket Casts

1. Copy the address above.
2. Open Pocket Casts.
3. Open Search and paste the address.
4. Open the show and tap Follow.

## Spotify

Spotify cannot add a private feed. A listener cannot paste an RSS URL. Spotify plays shows from its own catalog.

Say that in one line, and offer one app in its place. On an Apple device, offer Apple Podcasts. It is free, it is already installed, and it can follow a private URL. On any other device, offer Pocket Casts. It can follow a private URL, and following is free. When they agree, send the subscribe message for that app.
